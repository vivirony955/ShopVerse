# ShopVerse Partner Program

> For agencies, freelancers, and consultancies that build commerce stores for clients.

If you deploy stores for clients, ShopVerse gives you a hardened, source-available base — multi-warehouse inventory, a double-entry ledger, race-safe financial invariants, and a region-pluggable kernel — instead of rebuilding an order-management system per project. The partner program rewards you for bringing it to your clients.

**Apply / questions: vivironycrazy@gmail.com**

---

## Why partner

- **Own the stack you ship.** Source-available (BSL 1.1) — your clients keep their code and data, no per-seat SaaS lock-in.
- **A real OMS out of the box.** Multi-warehouse routing, returns/exchange, wallet ledger, fraud scoring, loyalty — features you'd otherwise assemble from a dozen plugins.
- **Region packs.** Tax, payment, and locale swap in per region (`shopverse-india`, `shopverse-us` ship today) — extend for your market.
- **You're the multiplier.** One agency deploying ShopVerse for its clients reaches far more stores than any single operator. The program is built around that.

## What partners get

| Benefit | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Revenue share** | A recurring share of the commercial-license fees from clients you bring on (above the $100k-GMV free threshold). |
| **Free white-label for dev/demo stores** | Build, demo, and prototype client stores without the badge or a per-store fee. |
| **"ShopVerse Certified" badge** | Listed as a certified partner; a trust signal for your prospects. |
| **Co-marketing** | Case studies, joint posts, and referrals from inbound leads in your region/vertical. |
| **Priority support** | A direct line for partner-deployed stores. |

## How it works

1. **Get in touch** — tell us your agency, region/vertical, and typical client profile.
2. **Build** — ship client stores on ShopVerse; use free white-label dev stores while building.
3. **Convert** — when a client store crosses $100k/yr GMV (or wants white-label / enterprise modules), it takes a commercial license; you earn the revenue share.
4. **Grow** — get listed, co-marketed, and prioritized.

See [COMMERCIAL_USAGE.md](COMMERCIAL_USAGE.md) for the licensing model and [docs/plugins/](docs/plugins/) for building client-specific plugins and region packs.
